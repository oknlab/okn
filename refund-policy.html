1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to all paid purchases made through OKNLAB's website or directly with our team, including monthly and annual subscription plans for proprietary software products, one-time fees for API access or credits, and project-based engagements for custom client work. By completing a purchase, you agree to the terms below.

2. Subscription Products

OKNLAB offers monthly and annual subscription plans for our proprietary AI tools and platforms.

Monthly Subscriptions

  • You may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of your initial payment or any renewal charge if you have not made substantial use of the service during that period
  • Refund requests submitted after 7 days will not be eligible for a refund for that billing period, but your subscription will not renew at the next cycle if you cancel
  • Cancellation is effective immediately — you retain access through the end of your current billing period

Annual Subscriptions

  • You may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of your initial annual payment if you are not satisfied with the service
  • After 14 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable in full; however, we may issue a pro-rated credit for the unused portion at our discretion
  • Renewals are treated the same as new annual purchases for refund eligibility purposes

Free Trials

  • If a free trial converts to a paid plan without explicit confirmation from you due to a billing error, we will issue a full refund upon request — no questions asked
  • Intentional conversions after the trial period are subject to the standard refund windows above

3. API Credits & One-Time Purchases

Pre-purchased API credits and one-time access fees are generally non-refundable once credits have been consumed or access has been granted. Exceptions apply in the following cases:

  • A technical fault on our end prevented you from using credits you purchased — we will restore the credits or issue a refund
  • You were charged the wrong amount due to a pricing error — we will correct the charge immediately
  • Unused credit balances at the time of account closure may be refunded at our discretion, subject to a minimum balance of USD $10.00

4. Custom Client Projects

Engagements for bespoke software development, design, or consulting are governed by the specific Statement of Work or contract agreed upon at project start. In the absence of a project-specific agreement, the following defaults apply:

  • Deposits (pre-delivery): Fully refundable if you cancel within 14 days of payment and no substantive work has commenced. Once active development begins, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • Milestone payments: Non-refundable once the corresponding milestone deliverable has been submitted for review.
  • Final payments: Non-refundable once final deliverables have been transferred to the client.
  • Quality disputes: If the delivered work materially fails to meet the agreed specification, we will revise the work at no additional cost or issue a partial refund commensurate with the unmet scope.

5. Non-Refundable Circumstances

The following situations are explicitly excluded from refund eligibility:

  • Dissatisfaction with AI-generated output that falls within the expected behavior and documented capabilities of the product
  • Failure to cancel a subscription before the next renewal date
  • Requests submitted outside the applicable refund window
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violations of our Terms of Service
  • Purchases made under promotional or discounted pricing, unless a separate guarantee was explicitly communicated
  • Third-party fees (payment processors, app stores, currency conversion) that are outside OKNLAB's control

6. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, contact us at hello@oknlab.slmail.me with the subject line "Refund Request" and include the following:

  • The email address associated with your account or purchase
  • The date and amount of the charge
  • A brief description of the reason for your request
  • Your order confirmation number or receipt, if available

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and aim to resolve it within 7 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and typically clear within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer or payment provider.

7. Billing Disputes & Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Most billing issues can be resolved faster through our support team, and chargebacks may result in immediate account suspension.

If you file a chargeback without first contacting us, we reserve the right to dispute the claim and provide your purchase records to the relevant financial institution.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated via email to active subscribers. Continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
